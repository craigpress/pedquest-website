## Where each fact came from
Benedetti 2023, Amplitude-Focused Trends, item 2 (Envelope trends), read verbatim from PMC10033183:
- "These trends are made by plotting the running average of either the median amplitude (envelope trend) or the maximum amplitude (peak envelope) of waveforms between 2 and 20 Hz, displaying amplitude variation over time."
- "Like aEEG, envelope trends may be displayed as a single graph or separated between the right and left hemispheres."
- "This trend can be helpful for detecting qEEG changes during a seizure, as amplitudes typically increase during a seizure, or other neuropathology that particularly affects EEG voltage."
The aEEG semilogarithmic display and the suppression ratio definition, used to construct distractors D and E, come from items 1 and 3 of the same section.

## Judgment calls
- Distractor E is the verbatim definition of a different trend on the same panel (suppression ratio), which is the most likely real-world confusion.
- Distractor C reverses the true relationship (the aEEG is classically the single-derivation trend), so a learner who has memorised a slogan rather than the construction is caught.
- The CICU post-arterial-switch setting is used only to justify long uninterrupted monitoring in an infant; no outcome claim is attached to it, so no cardiac-surgery reference is needed.
