## Where each number came from
Benedetti 2023 (Pediatr Neurol). Population: electronic survey sent to the Pediatric Neurocritical Care Research Group, the Pediatric Status Epilepticus Research Group, and intensivists in 16 Canadian pediatric intensive care units.
- 50 respondents from 39 institutions completed the survey; institutional response rate 53 % (39 of 74). 37 of 50 respondents (76 %) from the United States, 12 of 50 (24 %) from Canada.
- 22 of 39 institutions (56 %) used qEEG in their intensive care units.
- Use was associated with having a neurocritical care service, 200 or more neurocritical care consultations per year, 1500 or more intensive care admissions per year, and 4 or more intensive care EEGs per day (p < 0.05 for all).
- 24 of 26 users (92 %) endorsed that qEEG enhanced the care of children with acute neurologic injury.
- 22 of 26 (85 %) identified lack of qEEG training as a barrier.
- Training was required at 3 of 22 institutions (14 %); an established curriculum existed at 7 of 22 (32 %).

## Judgment calls
