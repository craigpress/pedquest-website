## Where each number came from
Kessler 2011. Population: 35 children prospectively enrolled and managed with a standard clinical therapeutic-hypothermia algorithm after cardiac arrest; primary outcome unfavorable short-term outcome, defined as Pediatric Cerebral Performance Category 4-6 at hospital discharge.
- Category 1 = continuous and reactive; category 2 = continuous but unreactive; category 3 = any degree of discontinuity, burst suppression or absent cerebral activity.
- Tracings during hypothermia: category 2 OR 10.7 (p = 0.023); category 3 OR 35 (p = 0.004).
- Tracings during normothermia: category 2 OR 27 (p = 0.006); category 3 OR 18 (p = 0.02).
Duff 2019: TTM 32-34 C followed by 36-37.5 C, or TTM 36-37.5 C alone, is reasonable for children who remain comatose after out-of-hospital or in-hospital arrest.

## Judgment calls
- Every distractor is a real odds ratio from the same paper (27, 35) or from the companion post-arrest cohort (3.63), so the item cannot be solved by spotting an implausible number.
- The explanation states that this is a single-center cohort of 35 children; the abstract reports no confidence intervals, so the point estimates are fragile and are labelled as such.
- Core temperature is stated in the vignette and encoded as a `temperature_change` event held at 33.0 C so the "during hypothermia" row of the paper is the applicable one.
