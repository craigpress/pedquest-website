## Where each number came from
Chahine 2023. Population: retrospective review of 73 neonates and children supported with extracorporeal membrane oxygenation and monitored with aEEG; median age 79 days (8-660), median weight 4.78 kg (3.24-10.02); background summarized as a score determined within the first 24 h in 3-hour periods; neurologic outcome by Pediatric Cerebral Performance Category at hospital discharge (32 favorable, 41 unfavorable).
- 24-hour aEEG background score above the reported threshold: sensitivity 44 %, specificity 97 %, positive predictive value 95 %, negative predictive value 57 %.
- Multivariable hazard ratio 6.1 (95 % CI 2.31-16.24, p = 0.001).
- The presence of seizures was not associated with neurologic outcome at hospital discharge.

Sansevere 2020. Population: 201 pediatric patients on ECMO, first 24 h of continuous EEG.
- Severely abnormal background in 25 of 201 (12 %), associated with death (sensitivity 0.23, specificity 0.97).

## Judgment calls
