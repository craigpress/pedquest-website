## Where each number came from
Abend 2011. Population: 100 critically ill non-neonatal children with acute encephalopathy who underwent continuous EEG using predetermined institutional monitoring indications and predefined EEG interpretation terminology; prospective enrollment and data collection.
- Electrographic seizures in 46; electrographic status epilepticus in 19; seizures exclusively nonconvulsive in 32.
- Younger age was the only clinical risk factor for seizure occurrence (p = 0.03).
- Of the patients with seizures, only 52 % had a seizure detected in the first hour of monitoring, whereas 87 % were detected within 24 hours.

## Judgment calls
- The 52 % and 87 % denominators are **patients with seizures**, not all monitored patients. The explanation states this explicitly, because the figure is frequently misquoted with the wrong denominator.
- The target seizure is placed at 40 minutes so that this item's own image sits inside the "first hour" group; the cluster over the following 22 hours illustrates the 87 % figure.
- The distractor feature is a suctioning-triggered muscle and movement burst at 15 minutes, annotated on the panel, so the learner has the bedside information a real reader would have.
- `target_event: 1` is 0-based: 0 = the artifact, 1 = the target seizure, 2 = the later cluster.
