## Where each definition came from
ACNS 2021 full text (PMC8135051), section B, read verbatim:
- "Attenuation: periods of lower voltage are >=10 uV but <50% of the higher voltage background."
- "Suppression: periods of lower voltage are <10 uV."
- "NOTE: If attenuations/suppressions are stimulus-induced, this is referred to as SI-attenuation or SI-suppression."
Benedetti 2023, Focal Cerebral Dysfunction Detection: persistent changes in asymmetry spectrograms relate to cerebral perfusion, increased or decreased, or to extracerebral pathology.

## Judgment calls
- **Answer region derives from the spec.** Three events are specified so the three declines in the vignette map one to one: index 0 is the seizure at 60 min with postictal_attenuation_s 60, index 1 the midazolam sedation_change at 180 min, index 2 the attenuation_transient at 300 min. target_event is 2, target_panel aeeg_L.
- **The 10 uV boundary is what makes this advanced rather than intermediate.** The spec sets attenuation_nadir_lower_margin_uv to 12 with a suppression threshold of 10, so the suppression ratio deliberately does not move during the attenuation. A learner who expects a suppression-ratio rise will click the wrong event, and the explanation names the reason.
- The clinical takeaway is deliberately soft (warrants immediate evaluation for a perfusion event) because no cited source in this item quantifies the yield of that evaluation in children; a numeric claim would be unsourced.
- Tolerance 0.03 on a 360-minute axis is about 11 minutes, comfortably separating three candidate events that are 120 minutes apart.
