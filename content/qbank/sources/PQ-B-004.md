## Where each number came from
Topjian 2016. Population: 128 children monitored within 1 day of return of spontaneous circulation after in-hospital or out-of-hospital arrest, **not** treated with therapeutic hypothermia.
- Background categories: normal 4 (3 %), slow-disorganized 58 (45 %), discontinuous or burst suppression 24 (19 %), attenuated-flat 42 (33 %).
- Reactive recording 46 (36 %); seizure during monitoring 20 (15 %).
- Per incrementally worse background score: odds of death 3.63 (95 % CI 2.18-6.0, p < 0.001); odds of unfavorable neurologic outcome 4.38 (2.51-7.17, p = 0.001).
- Absence of reactivity (p < 0.001) and seizures (p = 0.04) were associated with a worse background category.

## Judgment calls
- The vignette specifies targeted temperature management at 36.5 C so the cohort's "not treated with therapeutic hypothermia" condition holds and the odds ratios apply.
- Asking which feature is *most strongly* associated with death is defensible because the cited multivariable model used the graded background score, and reactivity and seizures were shown to co-vary with it rather than to be independent predictors.
