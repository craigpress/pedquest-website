## Where each fact came from
- "uses a density spectral array that displays greater rhythmicity as denser blue, calculated across sequential groupings of the 1- to 25-Hz frequency bands over time" — Benedetti 2023, Frequency-Focused Trends, item 4, read from PMC10033183.
- Non-ictal sources: "susceptible to highlighting nonictal rhythmic patterns, including sleep spindles, or the posterior dominant rhythm and repetitive artifacts (respiratory treatments or patting)" — same paragraph, verbatim list.
- Artifact reduction handles line-frequency (60 Hz in the United States, 50 Hz in Europe) and myogenic artifact well but is "less likely to be filtered" for patting and respiratory therapy artifact — Common qEEG Artifacts in the Pediatric ICU.
- "sensitivities ... ranged from 43% to 72%" across 562 seizures from 58 patients — Goenka 2018 abstract.

## Judgment calls
- **Vendor naming.** The rhythmicity spectrogram is a Persyst-specific trend and the source names the vendor. Per STYLE_GUIDE section 2 the vendor is kept out of the stem because the teaching point (rhythmicity is not specific for ictality) is generic, but the reference documenting the vendor-specific construction is cited.
- Goenka 2018 enrolled ages 5 to 64 years, mean 36; the explanation calls it a mixed-age cohort and never implies pediatric data.
- Distractor E (oscillation vest) is a genuinely correct mechanism in the right setting. It is excluded only by the explicit sentence stating that no respiratory treatment occurred in the displayed window, so that sentence is load-bearing and must survive editing.
