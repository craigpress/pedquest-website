## Where each fact came from
Benedetti 2023, Clinical Application of qEEG in the Pediatric ICU > Seizure Detection, read verbatim from PMC10033183:
- "A seizure will appear as a solid flame pattern. Power abruptly increases, peaks, and then attenuates during a seizure typically for both fast and slow frequencies, resulting in a flame-like appearance on the color spectrogram."
- "On an aEEG, if the seizure involves the electrodes used in the aEEG algorithm, the seizure appears as a sudden upward arched pattern because amplitude of the EEG waveform typically increases during the seizure."
- "The envelope trend will also peak during a seizure, as another amplitude-focused trend."
- "In the case of a focal seizure, the asymmetry index and spectrogram can be useful in highlighting laterality of ictal and postictal patterns."

## Judgment calls
- The conditional in the source ("if the seizure involves the electrodes used in the aEEG algorithm") is preserved in the explanation rather than dropped, because dropping it would teach an over-confident rule.
- Generalized seizures were chosen for the spec so that both hemispheric spectrograms and both aEEG channels respond, keeping the item at introductory level and avoiding a laterality sub-question.
- No accuracy statistics are quoted, so no performance reference is needed as primary; the review is the correct primary for a descriptive-signature item.
- Terminology: the vignette says electrographic seizure and states that no clinical movement was observed, per STYLE_GUIDE section 2 preference for ACNS terms over subclinical.
