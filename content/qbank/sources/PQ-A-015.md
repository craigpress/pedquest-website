## Where each number came from
Abend 2011 abstract, verbatim:
- "One hundred children were evaluated. Electrographic seizures occurred in 46 and electrographic status epilepticus occurred in 19. Seizures were exclusively nonconvulsive in 32."
- "Of patients with seizures, only 52% had seizures detected in the first hour of monitoring, while 87% were detected within 24 hours."
- "The only clinical risk factor for seizure occurrence was younger age (p=0.03)"; "Clinical features had little predictive value for seizure occurrence."
- Population: critically ill children, non-neonates, with acute encephalopathy.

## Judgment calls
- The ACNS definition of electrographic status epilepticus (a seizure for at least 10 continuous minutes, or at least 20 percent of any 60-minute period) is used to reject distractor C. That definition was read from the ACNS 2021 full text (PMC8135051, section D2) while building PQ-A-024; it is quoted here in the explanation only implicitly ("continuous or near-continuous ictal activity"), so no unsourced number is stated in this item. About 80 s in every 20 min is 6.7 percent, comfortably under the 20 percent threshold either way.
- The spec uses seizure_cluster with interval_min 20 across a 720-minute window, which yields the 36 events the explanation refers to; the arithmetic is derivable from the caption and the panel.
- The vignette rules out an observed clinical correlate and states that neuromuscular blockade was stopped 6 hours earlier, so the reader cannot attribute absence of clinical signs to paralysis.
