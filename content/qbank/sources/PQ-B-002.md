## Where each number came from
All from the al Naqeeb 1999 abstract. Population: 56 consecutive infants with neonatal encephalopathy (median gestation 40 weeks, range 35-42) plus 14 healthy term infants.
- Normal amplitude = upper margin > 10 uV and lower margin > 5 uV.
- Moderately abnormal amplitude = upper margin > 10 uV and lower margin <= 5 uV.
- Suppressed amplitude = upper margin < 10 uV and lower margin < 5 uV.
- Control median upper margin 37.5 uV (range 30-48); median lower margin 8 uV (range 6.5-11).
- Interobserver kappa 0.85 for amplitude (0.76 for seizures; not used).
- Overall sensitivity 0.93, specificity 0.70 against outcome at 18-24 months.

## Judgment calls
- The first draft used a *moderately abnormal* background, but the aEEG DSL renders the band from `pattern`, and a continuous band with a selectively depressed lower margin is not expressible unambiguously. The item was rebuilt around `pattern: CLV`, which the DSL renders deterministically and which maps onto the al Naqeeb *suppressed amplitude* category.
- Distractor B tests the continuity-versus-voltage confusion; C and D test single-margin reading. Only the correct option's two stated margin conditions both match the image.
- The <12 h subgroup figures (sens 1.0, spec 0.82, PPV 0.85, NPV 1, n = 24) were verified but not used, because the vignette is at 4 h and the subgroup is small.
- Option texts were shortened after a first pass to respect the style guide's 18-word ceiling.
