## Where each number came from
Payne 2014. Population: 259 infants and children admitted to a paediatric and a cardiac intensive care unit who underwent clinically ordered continuous video EEG over 3 years; median age 2.2 years; median monitoring 37 h (IQR 21-56).
- Seizures in 93 (36 %, 95 % CI 30-42); status epilepticus in 23 (9 %, 95 % CI 5-12).
- Mean maximum hourly seizure burden 15.7 % in those with neurologic decline versus 1.8 % without (p < 0.0001).
- Above a maximum burden of 20 % per hour (12 minutes) both the probability and the magnitude of neurologic decline rose sharply (p < 0.0001) across all diagnostic categories.
- Odds of decline rose 1.13 (95 % CI 1.05-1.21, p = 0.0016) per 1 % increase in maximum hourly burden.
- Seizure burden was not associated with mortality (OR 1.003, 0.99-1.02, p = 0.613).
