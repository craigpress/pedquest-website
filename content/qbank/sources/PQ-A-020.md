## Where each number came from
Din 2020 abstract, verbatim:
- 19 continuous raw EEG recordings; 379 seizures identified on raw EEG; six independent EEG experts.
- "ICTA-S detected 36.9%, NB detected 92.3%, Persyst 11 detected 75.9%, and Persyst 13 detected 74.4%, whereas electroencephalography experts identified 76.5% of seizures using color density spectral array and 73.7% using amplitude-integrated electroencephalography."
- "Daily false-positive rates averaged across all recordings were 4.7 with ICTA-S, 126.3 with NB, 5.1 with Persyst 11, 15.5 with Persyst 13, 1.7 with color density spectral array, and 1.5 with amplitude-integrated electroencephalography."
- "Although Persyst 13 displayed the highest sensitivity for seizure count and seizure burden detected, Persyst 11 exhibited the best trade-off between sensitivity and false-positive rate among all seizure detection algorithms."
- "These algorithms may have utility as early warning systems that prompt review of quantitative electroencephalography or raw electroencephalography tracings."
Lalgudi Ganesan 2018 is supporting and supplies the wider finding that trained critical care providers and EEG experts perform comparably on aEEG and CDSA; no number from it is quoted in this item.

## Judgment calls
- **Vendor names are removed from the stem and options and replaced by the sensitivity figures.** The options are therefore self-describing and the item cannot be answered by brand recognition; it also avoids reading as a product endorsement. The citation carries the product identities.
- Option D reproduces the true statement that one detector had the highest sensitivity for seizure count and burden, and is wrong only because its overall sensitivity was not highest and its false-positive rate was three times higher. That is the discriminating distractor.
- The six-patients-monitored detail in the vignette is what turns the daily false-positive rate into an answerable arithmetic judgement without requiring a number the learner has to recall.
- No claim is made about outcome benefit from detector use, because none of the cited studies measured it.
