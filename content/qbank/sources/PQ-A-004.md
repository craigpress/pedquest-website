## Where each fact came from
Benedetti 2023, Frequency-Focused Trends, item 3 (Asymmetry index and spectrogram), read verbatim from PMC10033183:
- absolute asymmetry index — "graphs the absolute difference (0-100%) between the hemispheres as a running average".
- relative asymmetry index sign convention — "index values (- 100 to 100%) on the y-axis: negative values when the left hemisphere has more power and positive values when the right hemisphere predominates".
- asymmetry spectrogram colour — "right hemisphere dominance for a specific frequency as red and left hemisphere dominance for a specific frequency as blue. The darker the color, the greater the hemispheric asymmetry"; y-axis "typically 0-20 Hz"; can be displayed holohemispherically or by region (anterior, posterior, temporal, parasagittal).
- extracerebral causes — Focal Cerebral Dysfunction Detection: asymmetry differences relate to cerebral perfusion, increased (seizure) or decreased (stroke, post-ictal state), "or extracerebral pathology (subdural hemorrhage or superficial scalp swelling)".
