## Where each number came from
Din 2020 abstract, verbatim:
- "Of the 379 seizures identified on raw electroencephalography, ICTA-S detected 36.9%, NB detected 92.3%, Persyst 11 detected 75.9%, and Persyst 13 detected 74.4%, whereas electroencephalography experts identified 76.5% of seizures using color density spectral array and 73.7% using amplitude-integrated electroencephalography."
- "Daily false-positive rates averaged across all recordings were 4.7 with ICTA-S, 126.3 with NB, 5.1 with Persyst 11, 15.5 with Persyst 13, 1.7 with color density spectral array, and 1.5 with amplitude-integrated electroencephalography."
- 19 continuous raw EEG recordings; six independent EEG experts.
- "These algorithms may have utility as early warning systems that prompt review of quantitative electroencephalography or raw electroencephalography tracings."

## Judgment calls
- **Answer region derives from the spec.** target_event index 1 is the seizure at onset_min 143.1 with onset_region left_temporal; index 0 is the ECG artifact on T4 and T6. target_panel is "page" because this is an eeg_page.
- Tolerance 0.05, wider than the panel items, because a chain on a raw page occupies a taller band than a point on a trend line.
- The item deliberately places the ECG artifact in a right-sided chain and includes a displayed ECG channel, so the discriminating evidence (one-to-one correspondence with the ECG) is visible on the image rather than only stated.
- Vendor names appear in the source but not in the item; the explanation reports the four algorithm results anonymously as percentages with their false-positive rates, which is enough for the teaching point and avoids an implicit product endorsement.
