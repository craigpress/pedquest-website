## Where each number came from
Topjian 2013. Population: 200 non-neonatal children admitted to a PICU with acute encephalopathy, prospective observational, all monitored with continuous EEG.
- Seizures in 84 (42 %): electrographic seizures 41 (20.5 %), electrographic status epilepticus 43 (21.5 %).
- Deaths 36 (18 %); worsening of Pediatric Cerebral Performance Category from pre-admission to PICU discharge 88 (44 %).
- Multivariable model adjusted for age, acute neurologic disorder category, prior neurodevelopmental status, sex and EEG background category: electrographic status epilepticus -> mortality OR 5.1 (95 % CI 1.4-18, p = 0.01); PCPC worsening OR 17.3 (3.7-80, p < 0.001).
- Electrographic seizures -> mortality OR 1.3 (0.3-5.1, p = 0.74); PCPC worsening OR 1.2 (0.4-3.9, p = 0.77).

## Judgment calls
- Option E was originally drafted as "discontinuous background alone" and was changed to "interictal epileptiform discharges without seizures", because EEG background category was a *covariate* in the cited model rather than a tested null exposure; calling it unassociated would have been unsupported.
- Options C and D name ictal-interictal continuum patterns that the image spec deliberately does not contain (generalized symmetric status epilepticus with a flat asymmetry index), so the image, not just the prose, excludes them.
