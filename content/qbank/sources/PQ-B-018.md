## Where each number came from
This item makes **no quantitative claim**. All five option texts are the indication categories stated in the Herman 2015 abstract.
- The panel **recommends** continuous EEG for diagnosis of nonconvulsive seizures, nonconvulsive status epilepticus and other paroxysmal events, and for assessment of the efficacy of therapy for seizures and status epilepticus.
- The panel **suggests** continuous EEG for identification of ischemia in patients at high risk for cerebral ischemia; for assessment of level of consciousness in patients receiving intravenous sedation or pharmacologically induced coma; and for prognostication in patients after cardiac arrest.
- For each indication the statement describes the patient populations involved, the utility of video and quantitative EEG trends, the suggested timing and duration of monitoring, and the suggested frequency of review and interpretation.
