## Where each number came from
Shellhaas 2007. Population: 851 neonatal seizures identified on 125 conventional EEGs from infants of 34 to 50 weeks conceptional age. The single C3 to C4 channel was used to simulate the raw signal from which aEEG is derived; six neonatologists with varying aEEG experience read the derived aEEG.
- At least one seizure was visible in C3 to C4 on 94 % of the records; 78 % of all individual seizures appeared in that channel.
- Seizures were briefer and lower in amplitude in C3 to C4 than on the conventional recording.
- Neonatologists identified seizures on 22 to 57 % of the 125 records and detected 12 to 38 % of the 851 individual seizures.
- On multivariate analysis, detection correlated with appearance in C3 to C4, seizure duration, seizure amplitude, seizure count per hour, and reader experience.

Wusthoff 2025 (ACNS neonatal indications guideline): an initial search yielded 18,167 results distilled to 217 articles; the quality of evidence for most priority questions was rated very low and the recommendations are conditional.
