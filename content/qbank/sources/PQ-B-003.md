## Where each number came from
Osredkar 2005, population 171 term newborns with hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy over a 10-year consecutive series:
- Sleep-wake cycling in 95.4 % of survivors, 8.1 % of those who died.
- Median hours from birth to onset of cycling by HIE grade: 7 (I), 33 (II), 62 (III).
- Cycling delayed by 30.5 h in newborns with seizure discharges.
- Median Griffiths developmental quotient differed by 8.5 points across the 36-hour cutoff.
- The 36-hour cutoff classified good versus poor outcome correctly in 82 %.
Thoresen 2010 (n = 74): never developing cycling always predicted poor outcome.

## Judgment calls
- The 8.5-point figure is a difference in *median* developmental quotients; the option says "about 8.5 points higher" rather than rounding to 8 or 9.
- Options D and E are directionally plausible alternatives (survival-only effect, motor-only effect) rather than numeric variants, so the item is not solvable by arithmetic elimination.
- The vignette states sedation stopped 12 h before the window, so emerging cycling cannot be read as a drug-washout artefact.
