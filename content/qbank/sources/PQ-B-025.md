## Where each number came from
- The only numeric claim is from Payne 2014: in 259 critically ill children, higher maximum hourly seizure burden was independently associated with neurologic decline (OR 1.13 per 1 % increase, 95 % CI 1.05-1.21, p = 0.0016).
- **No duration is given for the post-ictal attenuation**, because no verified pediatric source for a typical recovery interval was found. The explanation instead relies on three qualitative discriminators — bilaterality, temporal locking to seizure offset, and reversibility within the displayed window — all of which are fixed by the image spec (`attenuation_transient` at 60 min, `duration_min: 55`, `side: both`, `depth_pct: 65`).
- ACNS 2021 is used only for the terms *attenuation* and *suppression*; no threshold or percentage is attributed to it.

## Judgment calls
- The attenuation is specified as bilateral in the spec so that options B and E, both of which require lateralization, are excluded by the rendered image rather than only by the prose.
- The lorazepam dose is annotated at the same minute as the seizure offset, so the drug and the post-ictal state are temporally confounded in this vignette. That is realistic, and it is why the vignette specifies that **no continuous sedative infusion** was started: a single benzodiazepine dose does not produce a sustained plateau, and the trends are already reversing by the end of the window. A reviewer may wish to move the annotation 5 minutes earlier to make the point cleaner.
- Payne 2014 is cited as primary rather than supporting because the bedside takeaway (keep quantifying burden after status is treated) rests on it, not merely on the terminology document.
