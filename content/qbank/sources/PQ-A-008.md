## Where each fact came from
Benedetti 2023, Common qEEG Artifacts in the Pediatric ICU, read from PMC10033183:
- "Most qEEG software offers artifact reduction algorithms, which perform well in removal of artifact from electrical artifact (60 Hz in the United States, 50 Hz in Europe) and myogenic artifact."
- "Less likely to be filtered by artifact reduction are rhythmic patterns such as patting and respiratory therapy artifact, and both can be mistaken for seizures if the raw EEG and video are not reviewed."
- Figure 9a legend describes artifact in the 13 to 14 Hz frequency range from a high-frequency chest wall oscillation vest in a 13-year-old. ## Judgment calls
- The vignette gives the vest schedule in the nursing flowsheet so the answer is derivable without recalling the paper; the item tests reasoning about processing, not memory of a frequency.
- The age (13 years) and the 13 Hz band match the described clinical scenario in the source, but the vignette is a composite (cystic fibrosis, respiratory failure, new encephalopathy) and shares no identifying detail with any real case.
- Option E is the nihilistic distractor and is false because artifact reduction demonstrably works on two of the three categories.
