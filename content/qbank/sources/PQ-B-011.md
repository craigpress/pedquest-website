## Where each number came from
Kharoshankaya 2016. Population: 47 full-term neonates with hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy at a single maternity hospital, 2003-2006 (pre-hypothermia era) and 2009-2012 (hypothermia era); continuous EEG for a median of 57.1 h (IQR 33.5-80.5); outcome at 24-48 months by Bayley III or Griffiths, with cerebral palsy or epilepsy counted as abnormal.
- Electrographic seizures in 29 of 47 (62 %); abnormal outcome in 25 of 47 (53 %).
- Presence of seizures per se not associated with abnormal outcome (p = 0.126).
- Total seizure burden greater than 40 minutes: OR 9.56 (95 % CI 2.43-37.67, p = 0.001).
- Maximum hourly seizure burden greater than 13 minutes per hour: OR 8.00 (2.06-31.07, p = 0.003).
- Controlling for electrographic HIE grade or treatment with hypothermia did not change the direction of the relationship.

## Judgment calls
- The image spec was corrected after a first draft. Four-minute seizures every 20-25 minutes would have produced only 8-12 minutes in the worst hour, contradicting the stem. The final spec has 16 seizures of 3.5 minutes at 15-minute intervals across a 4-hour window, giving exactly four per hour and 14 minutes per hour in every hour, with the last seizure ending before the window closes. Verified programmatically.
- Distractor D (20 minutes per hour) is a deliberate cross-population trap: 20 percent of an hour is 12 minutes and comes from a mixed PICU cohort, not from a neonatal study.
- Distractor E (40 minutes per hour) is the *total* burden threshold from the same paper misapplied to a single hour.
