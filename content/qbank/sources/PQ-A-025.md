## Where each statement came from
ACNS 2021 full text (PMC8135051), section B item 5, Reactivity, read verbatim:
- "Change in cerebral EEG activity to stimulation: This may include change in voltage or frequency, including attenuation of activity."
- "Strength and/or nature of stimulus should be noted, and a standard protocol of testing reactivity with multiple escalating stimuli is strongly encouraged."
- "Appearance of muscle activity or eye blink artifacts does not qualify as reactive."
- Categories listed: Reactive; Unreactive; SIRPIDs-only; Unclear, "typically used when testing may have not been adequate".
- "NOTE: It is suggested that if an EEG is unreactive after one round of stimulation, a second round of standardized noxious stimulation should be performed to confirm the finding and should be applied with the patient in their nonstimulated state. If unreactive and the patient is on sedatives or paralytics, we suggest including this important caveat in the impression."
Herman 2015 part I abstract confirms the consensus suggestion to use continuous EEG for assessment of level of consciousness in patients receiving intravenous sedation or pharmacologically induced coma.

## Judgment calls
- **Both plausible wrong directions are covered.** Options B and C call the record reactive on the basis of artifact; option D calls it unreactive after a single round. The terminology forbids both, leaving one defensible answer.
- Option E is attractive because Unclear is a genuine ACNS category; it is wrong only because abandoning testing is not what the document suggests. The rationale says exactly that rather than dismissing the category.
- The vignette states that neuromuscular blockade has been off for 24 hours and a low-dose sedative infusion is running, so the sedation caveat rather than paralysis is the relevant footnote and the reader is not distracted by a paralysis question.
- No numeric performance claim appears in this item, so no accuracy statistic required sourcing.
- The image.spec pairs a stimulation event with a simultaneous high-intensity emg artifact on the four temporal electrodes, so the myogenic nature of the response is rendered deterministically rather than only asserted in the stem.
