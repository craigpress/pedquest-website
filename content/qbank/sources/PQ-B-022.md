## Where each number came from
Sansevere 2020. Population: single-center retrospective study of 201 pediatric patients on extracorporeal membrane oxygenation, using the first 24 hours of continuous EEG, collated with imaging, ECMO type and outcome.
- Severely abnormal background in 25 of 201 (12 %), associated with death (sensitivity 0.23, specificity 0.97).
- Electrographic seizures in 33 of 201 (16 %), occurring at a median of 3.2 hours (IQR 0.6-20.3) after continuous EEG commencement; higher seizure burden associated with death.
- Seizures were always associated with ipsilateral injury (p = 0.006) but occurred in only about one-third of cases with abnormal imaging.
- Among 28 patients with an isolated hemisphere lesion, right carotid cannulation was associated with right hemisphere lesions and ascending aortic cannulation with left hemisphere lesions (OR 0.29, 95 % CI 0.08-0.98, p = 0.03).

## Judgment calls
- The pump artifact is encoded as a continuous `ecmo_pump` event spanning the whole 6-hour window so that it renders as a fixed horizontal stripe. That fixed, non-evolving, bilateral appearance is the discriminator the item teaches; the patting artifact at 300 minutes is a second, briefer distractor.
- `target_event: 1` is 0-based: 0 = the pump artifact, 1 = the target seizure, 2 = the patting artifact.
- The cannulation side in the vignette (right carotid) and the imaged infarct side (right middle cerebral artery) are chosen to match the paper's reported association, and the seizure is specified as `right_hemisphere`, so the ipsilateral rule holds in the rendered image.
