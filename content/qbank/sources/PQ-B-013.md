## Where each number came from
Naim 2015. Population: quality-improvement cohort over 18 months at a single center; 161 of 172 eligible neonates (94 %) underwent continuous EEG after cardiac surgery with cardiopulmonary bypass; tracings interpreted with standardized ACNS terminology.
- Electrographic seizures in 13 neonates (8 %), beginning at a median of 20 hours after return to the intensive care unit.
- Seizures were electroencephalogram only in 11 of the 13 (85 %); status epilepticus in 8 of 13 (62 %).
- Neonates with all types of congenital heart disease had seizures.
- Delayed sternal closure and longer deep hypothermic circulatory arrest duration were each associated with increased seizure risk in separate multivariable models.
- Mortality was higher among neonates with than without seizures, 38 % versus 3 % (p < 0.001).

- `target_event: 1` is the **0-based** index into `image.spec.events`, which are listed in chronological order: 0 = chest physiotherapy artifact at 300 min, 1 = the target seizure, 2 = the following cluster.
